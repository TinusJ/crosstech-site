About us

From mobile studio to AI-first software company.

CrossTech was founded in 2019 as a mobile app development studio. The tools changed, and so did we: today we build software of every shape — web, cloud, mobile — with artificial intelligence at the centre of how we design it and what we deliver.

🎯

Our mission

To build superior, intelligent software that solves real problems — and to keep learning and improving on our craft so every product we ship is better than the last.

🤝

Our approach

Long-term relationships built on trust and reliability. We work with you through the whole product life cycle — from first idea to running system — and no query goes unanswered.

🚀

Our vision

To be a leading AI software studio — proving that world-class intelligent software can be built anywhere — and to keep raising that bar, globally.

What we believe

AI is a tool, not a slogan.

Honest engineering

If a problem doesn't need a model, we won't force one in. When it does, we build it properly: evaluated, monitored, and owned end to end.

Ship, then keep shipping

Software isn't done at launch. We stay involved — supporting, measuring and improving — for as long as the product lives.
