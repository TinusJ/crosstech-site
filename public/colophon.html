About this site

The site you're reading is our proof.

Most agency websites are heavy, script-laden and quietly watching you. This one is the opposite — deliberately. We say we build lean, honest software; this page is where you can check that claim against something we actually shipped. Every statement below is verifiable from your browser or from our public repository.

How it's built

Hand-written. Shipped verbatim.

No pipeline of tools between what we write and what you receive — the files we commit are the files your browser gets.

✍️

Written by hand

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, typed out and read in full by the people who ship it. No frameworks, no template soup — view the source of this very page and you'll see markup a human can actually follow.

📦

Zero build step

Nothing is compiled, bundled, minified or transpiled. What sits in the repository is byte-for-byte what gets deployed — which means there's nowhere for cruft to hide.

🚀

Public repo, CI deploy

The whole site lives in a public GitHub repository and deploys to Firebase Hosting automatically on every push. Anyone — including you — can read the source of this exact page. Browse the repo →

Why it matters to you

The way we build our site is the way we'd build yours.

⚡

Fast everywhere

Pages this lean load fast on any connection — a hotel wifi, a train, an old phone. Speed isn't a plugin you add later; it's what's left when you don't add the bloat in the first place.

🔒

Nothing collected

We collect nothing about visitors beyond what you choose to type in the contact form — and even that is handled by our own pipeline: your message gets a reference number and an instant auto-reply, with no third-party form service in between. See how that pipeline is built →

🧹

Same philosophy, for clients

Lean where lean wins, heavier tools only when they earn their place. The discipline you can inspect on this page is the same one we bring to the software we build for you.

Honest limits: lean doesn't mean magic. This site uses the browser's native capabilities rather than reinventing them, and it isn't entirely self-contained: the contact page loads Google's Firebase library so your message can be delivered. Everything else — stylesheet, script, images, and the two font families (Inter and Space Grotesk, self-hosted since September 2026 so no request goes to Google Fonts) — is served from our own domain. You can confirm all of it in your browser's network tab.
