Questions, answered honestly.

What does the free AI Opportunity Audit involve?

A 30-minute call about how your business actually runs — where the hours go, which systems you use. Within a week you get a short written automation map: what's worth automating first, roughly what it takes, and what we'd skip. No obligation attached.

When is AI the wrong answer?

More often than the hype admits. If a workflow is rare, constantly changing, or a mistake in it is expensive, plain software — or no software — can beat AI. If that's your case, the audit will say exactly that.

How long until something actually works?

A first automation or AI connector typically takes two to four weeks to working software. Bigger product builds are scoped in weeks, not quarters — you'll see something you can click early and often.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. Builds are scoped after it — fixed-price where the shape is clear, short iterations where it isn't — and you approve the scope before anything is billed. No surprises is a feature we take seriously.

What is MCP, in plain terms?

An open standard that lets AI assistants work with your existing apps — read from them, act in them — through a connector you control. Your systems stay exactly where they are; the AI gets a safe, permissioned door in.

Do we have to replace our current software?

No. Connecting and extending what you already run is usually the whole point — most of our work plugs into existing systems rather than replacing them.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The code, the connectors, the infrastructure — handed over, documented, running in your own accounts.

Is our data safe with AI in the loop?

You decide exactly what any AI can see and do — access is permissioned and logged, and we design with the least access that does the job. Data handling is agreed explicitly before anything is connected.

What happens after launch?

We stay. Monitoring, updates, security patches and steady improvement — with real humans answering when something breaks.
